FAQs About Learning the Quran Online

Answers about Tajweed lessons, children’s readiness, female teacher requests, monthly fees and getting started.

Can I learn the Quran online with Tajweed?

Yes. In live, one-to-one classes, your tutor listens as you read and helps you apply Tajweed rules, including letter sounds and pronunciation. You need a laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection and a quiet space.

How do beginners and new Muslims start Quran lessons?

Start with a free trial so we can discuss your current level and goals. Beginners and reverts can begin with Noorani Qaida before moving to Quran reading and recitation. Lessons are available in English, Arabic and Urdu.

Can I request female Quran teachers?

Yes. Women and parents seeking a female Quran teacher can message us on WhatsApp before the trial. We will do our best to accommodate your preference, depending on tutor availability and a suitable lesson time.

How long is the free trial Quran class?

The free trial is usually 20 to 30 minutes. It gives you time to meet a tutor, discuss your reading level and experience the lesson format before choosing a course. No payment or credit card is required.

When should children start learning the Quran?

Some children are ready around age four, while others need more time. Interest, attention span and comfort with a teacher matter more than a fixed age. Use the trial to discuss an age-appropriate starting point and lesson pace.

Are online Quran classes suitable for adults?

Yes. Adults can learn Quran online from beginner level, improve recitation with Tajweed or study Hifz and Tafseer. Flexible class times help you fit Quran lessons around work and family life, with a pace suited to your current level.

What are the pricing plans for Quran tutoring?

Standard monthly plans cost USD 30 for eight or USD 40 for sixteen 30-minute lessons. Tafseer costs USD 60 for eight or USD 100 for sixteen lessons.

Can I reschedule or cancel a Quran class?

Message your tutor or Quran Hub on WhatsApp as early as possible if your schedule changes. The team can help you discuss available lesson times and explain the rescheduling or cancellation arrangements that apply to your plan.
